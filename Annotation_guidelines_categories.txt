Annotation Guidelines

POSITIVE
Words expressing approval, desirability, or aesthetic/sensory pleasure. Include subjective judgments of approval (pleasant, exquisite, good).

NEGATIVE
Words expressing disapproval, unpleasantness, or sensory discomfort. Include undesirable qualities (worst, offensive, nasty)

SOURCE_BASED
Words that reference concrete, specific substances or objects as flavor/sensation sources.

QUANTITY/INTENSITY
Words indicating degree, scope, or deviation from norm, e.g., comparative/superlative terms, or precision markers.

NEUTRAL
Words that describe without clear positive/negative judgment or context-dependent meaning.
